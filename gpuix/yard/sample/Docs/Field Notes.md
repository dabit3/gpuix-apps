# Field Notes: Lake District, October

Three days walking the western fells. Photos are in `../Photos`.

## Day 1: Buttermere
- Left Gatesgarth at 08:10, light rain clearing by ten.
- **Haystacks** summit by noon; Innominate Tarn was glass-still.
- Descended via Scarth Gap. 14.2 km, 820 m ascent.
